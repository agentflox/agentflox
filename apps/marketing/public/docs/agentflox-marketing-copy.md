## Notes for Design & Content Teams

1. **Lead every section with the outcome**, then back it up with the feature — "ship faster" before "30+ views," not the reverse.
2. **Keep the OS narrative intact.** Individual modules should always gesture back to "one system," even on single-feature pages.
3. **Screenshots should show view diversity** — a board next to a Gantt next to a map reads as range; three boards in a row reads as sameness.
4. **Don't overload one page with all four AI storylines.** Agents, Workforces, Tools, and Marketplace each deserve their own page and their own hero; cramming all four into one hero dilutes the pitch.
5. **Hold off on hard numbers** (customer counts, agent counts, uptime stats) until they're verified — these drafts use capability language instead of unverified social proof, on purpose.
